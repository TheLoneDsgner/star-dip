Who we are

Development Starts With People

STAR DEVELOPMENT IMPACT PARTNERS (StarDIP) is a Nigerian development organization working to build stronger communities through inclusive, locally driven solutions.

We bring people, expertise, and partnerships together to address challenges affecting health, livelihoods, and the environment.

Our Mission

To strengthen communities and drive sustainable development through inclusive, evidence-informed solutions.

Our Vision

A healthier, more resilient, and inclusive society where every community can thrive.

Our Purpose

Creating Change That Lasts

We believe communities thrive when people have the knowledge, opportunities, and resources to shape their future.

Our work connects health, livelihoods, and environmental sustainability to create lasting impact.

Our Core Values

What guide us

People First

We put people's needs, dignity, and well-being at the centre of our work.

Locally Led

We build solutions around community priorities and local knowledge.

Evidence-Informed

We use research, data, and learning to guide our decisions.

Partnership-Driven

We collaborate to create better, more sustainable outcomes.

Sustainability-Focused

We build capacity for lasting change beyond individual projects.

Results-Oriented

We measure progress, learn, and continuously improve.
