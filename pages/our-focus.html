One Health

Healthy People. Healthy Animals. Healthy Environments.

One Health

Healthy People. Healthy Animals. Healthy Environments.

The health of people, animals, plants, and the environment is closely connected. StarDIP promotes an integrated approach to community well-being by bringing these areas together through awareness, prevention, and cross-sector collaboration.

Our focus

We support community-level actions that help prevent shared health risks and promote healthier environments.

Our areas of focus include:

  • Community One Health awareness and education.
  • Prevention of diseases that spread between animals and humans.
  • Animal and livestock health awareness.
  • Food safety and environmental health education.
  • Water, sanitation, and hygiene promotion.
  • Climate and health awareness.
  • Antimicrobial resistance awareness.
  • Collaboration among health, agriculture, and environmental stakeholders.

Why it matters

Health challenges do not exist in isolation. Issues affecting animals, food systems, water, and the environment can also affect human health and community livelihoods. Addressing these connections helps communities become better informed and prepared.

One gealth goal

To strengthen community awareness, encourage preventive practices, and support collaboration towards healthier people, animals, and environments.

Health & Community Well-being

Healthier Communities, Better Quality of Life.

We support initiatives that improve access to health information, preventive services, and community-based health interventions, particularly for underserved populations.

Our work focuses on strengthening health awareness, promoting healthy behaviours, and connecting individuals and communities to appropriate health services.

Our focus

We support efforts that promote preventive healthcare and improve community access to essential health information.

Our areas of focus include:

  • Primary healthcare awareness.
  • Community health education.
  • Disease prevention and health promotion.
  • Health screening and referrals.
  • Maternal and child health awareness.
  • Adolescent health education.
  • Nutrition and healthy living.
  • Water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH).
  • Community health workforce development.

Why it matters

Access to reliable health information and preventive services helps people make informed decisions about their well-being. Stronger community health awareness also supports early action and better connections to appropriate healthcare.

Our goal

To contribute to healthier communities by promoting awareness, prevention, and improved access to essential health information and services.

Pad Without Price

Because Dignity Should Never Have a Price.

Pad Without Price

Because Dignity Should Never Have a Price.

Pad Without Price is a StarDIP initiative focused on improving access to menstrual products and menstrual health education for girls and young women.

We believe every girl deserves to manage her period safely, confidently, and with dignity. Our work combines access to essential menstrual products with education that challenges stigma and promotes informed menstrual health practices.

Our focus

We go beyond product distribution to promote knowledge, confidence, and supportive environments for menstrual health.

Our areas of focus include:

  • Distribution of free menstrual pads.
  • Menstrual health and hygiene education.
  • Puberty education for girls.
  • Addressing stigma and misconceptions around menstruation.
  • School and community outreach.
  • Education for parents and caregivers.
  • Engagement with teachers and community leaders.
  • Referrals to appropriate health services where necessary.

Why it matters

Limited access to menstrual products and accurate information can affect girls' confidence, well-being, and participation in everyday activities, including education. Creating supportive environments helps girls manage menstruation without unnecessary barriers.

Our goal

To help girls and young women access menstrual products and reliable information so they can manage their periods with confidence and dignity.

Agriculture & Sustainable Livelihood

Growing Food. Strengthening Livelihoods. Building Resilience.

Agriculture & Sustainable Livelihood

Growing Food. Strengthening Livelihoods. Building Resilience.

Agriculture plays an important role in food security, economic opportunity, and community resilience. StarDIP supports sustainable and climate-smart agricultural practices that help farmers improve productivity, strengthen livelihoods, and build more resilient food systems.

Our focus

We promote practical agricultural knowledge, stronger farming systems, and opportunities for sustainable livelihoods.

Our areas of focus include:

  • Farmer training and agricultural extension support.
  • Climate-smart and sustainable farming practices.
  • Smallholder farmer development.
  • Women and youth participation in agriculture.
  • Agribusiness and agricultural entrepreneurship.
  • Agricultural value-chain strengthening.
  • Food security initiatives.
  • Market access and linkages.
  • Post-harvest management.
  • Livelihood diversification.

Why it matters

Farmers and agricultural communities face challenges that affect productivity, income, and food availability. Access to practical knowledge, resources, and stronger market opportunities can help address these challenges.

Our goal

To support productive and resilient agricultural livelihoods while contributing to food security and sustainable community development.

Youth Development & Empowerment

Equipping Young People for Opportunity.

Young people are important drivers of innovation, leadership, and community development. StarDIP supports initiatives that equip young people with the knowledge, skills, and opportunities to participate meaningfully in society and build productive futures.

Our focus

We promote personal development, employability, entrepreneurship, and youth participation.

Our areas of focus include:

  • Life skills development.
  • Employability and career readiness.
  • Entrepreneurship and business development.
  • Digital skills and technology awareness.
  • Leadership development.

  • Youth health education.
  • Community participation and volunteering.
  • Innovation and skills development programmes.

Many young people face barriers to accessing relevant skills, mentorship, and economic opportunities. Creating pathways for learning and participation can help them unlock their potential and contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Women, Girls & Youth

Creating Opportunities. Advancing Dignity. Strengthening Voices.

We support initiatives that improve access to information, healthcare, livelihoods, and opportunities for women, girls, and young people.

  • Menstrual health and hygiene.

Climate & Environment

Promoting environmental sustainability and community resilience.

A healthy environment is essential to community well-being, livelihoods, and sustainable development. StarDIP promotes environmental awareness and sustainable practices that encourage communities to understand, respond to, and reduce environmental challenges.

Our focus

We encourage responsible environmental practices and community participation in building a more sustainable future.

Our areas of focus include:

  • Environmental awareness and education.
  • Climate change awareness.
  • Community-led environmental initiatives.
  • Sustainable natural resource management.
  • Waste management and responsible disposal.
  • Environmental health promotion.
  • Climate-smart agricultural practices.
  • Community resilience and adaptation.
  • Stakeholder collaboration on environmental sustainability.

Why it matters

Environmental challenges can affect public health, food systems, livelihoods, and the resources communities depend on. Building awareness and encouraging sustainable practices are important steps towards reducing these risks.

Our goal

To support environmentally responsible communities that are better equipped to adapt to environmental challenges and protect the resources they depend on.

Capacity Building

Limited skills, resources, and institutional systems can affect how effectively development initiatives are designed and delivered. Strengthening local capacity helps individuals and organizations respond to challenges and sustain progress.

Our goal

To strengthen people, institutions, and community systems with the knowledge and capabilities needed to drive sustainable development.
